Privacy Policy for Mila Creator Agent

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Mila Creator Agent is an application used to assist with managing communications for the Instagram professional account @themilastory.

Information We Access

The application may access information provided through the Instagram API, including:

  • Instagram account identifiers and basic profile information;
  • Direct messages sent to the connected Instagram account;
  • Comments, mentions, and related interaction data;
  • Conversation metadata necessary to manage and respond to messages.

How We Use Information

Information is used only to:

  • receive and respond to Instagram messages;
  • manage conversations and interactions;
  • generate appropriate automated or AI-assisted responses;
  • maintain conversation context where necessary;
  • operate, secure, and improve the Mila Creator Agent.

AI Processing

Some message content may be processed by artificial intelligence service providers for the purpose of generating replies. Information is shared only as necessary to provide this functionality and is not sold to advertisers or data brokers.

Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the application. Access to stored data is restricted to authorized systems and administrators.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may only be shared with service providers required to operate the application, such as hosting, automation, database, and artificial intelligence providers.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the application, maintain conversation context, comply with applicable requirements, or resolve technical and security issues.

Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with their interactions with Mila Creator Agent by contacting us at the email address below.

Requests should include sufficient information to identify the relevant Instagram conversation. After verification, applicable stored information will be deleted within a reasonable period unless retention is required by law.

Third-Party Platforms

Mila Creator Agent uses services provided by Meta and Instagram and may use additional third-party infrastructure and AI providers. Their processing of data is subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.
